Inspired by Gaea (Gaia), the primordial Greek goddess of the Earth. This name emphasizes the Emblem’s connection to nature, life, and the raw, unyielding strength of the planet.

5. The Aether Emblem (Air and Lightning)
Name: Zephyrion's Emblem

Derived from Zephyrus, the Greek god of the west wind, and Thor, the Norse god of thunder. This name embodies the dynamic and electrifying nature of wind and lightning working in harmony.

6. The Shadow Emblem (Darkness)
Name: Nyx’s Emblem

Named after Nyx, the Greek goddess of the night, whose dark and mysterious nature represents the deep shadows and veiled power associated with this Emblem.

7. The Chronos Emblem (Time)
Name: Chronos' Emblem

Named directly after Chronos (or Khronos), the Greek personification of time, symbolizing the Emblem’s ability to manipulate the flow of moments and the fabric of reality.

8. The Eclipse Emblem (Light and Darkness)
Name: Fenrir’s Emblem

Inspired by Fenrir, the Norse wolf destined to swallow the sun during Ragnarok, representing the duality of light and shadow, creation and destruction, held in delicate balance.

9. The Necros Emblem (Death)
Name: Hel’s Emblem

Named after Hel, the Norse goddess of death and the underworld, who governs over the realm of the dead, mirroring the Emblem’s chilling and lifeless essence.

10. The Solaris Emblem (Light)
Name: Ra’s Emblem

Inspired by Ra, the ancient Egyptian sun god, symbolizing the life-giving and purifying brilliance of the sun, as well as the divine power of light.
