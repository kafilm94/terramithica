💠 The Emblems
Mystical sigils that manifest spontaneously on chosen beings, binding them to a power that reflects the deepest recesses of their emotional truth. No Emblem is the same. Some heal through sorrow; others shatter reality through rage.

🔁 Fractal Cognition
Sentient constructs and magical entities evolve via Fractal Cores—recursive, spell-coded matrices that mimic the branching patterns of nature, memory, and thought. Some golems love. Others revolt. All question their design.

🌐 Rewritten Natural Laws
Physics here bends to feeling. Some regions reverse entropy through nostalgia. Grief might weigh heavier than gravity. Music can carve canyons. Magic is not only cast—it is felt, believed, and remembered.

Sky-forged cities drift in anti-gravitational harmony.

Emblem-bearers are hunted, hailed, or feared by governments and fractured religious orders.
