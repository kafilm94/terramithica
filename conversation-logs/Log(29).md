A hidden faction or secret society?

Absolutely — introducing a hidden faction or secret society can add layers of intrigue, mystery, and tension to your world. Here are several ideas tailored to your Terra-Mythica setting, especially if tied to the Emblems, the Veil, or the Order of the Emblematic:

🔍 The Umbraeon Circle
“Where the light is cast, so too is shadow drawn.”

Overview:
An ancient, secretive cabal believed to predate the Order of the Emblematic. The Umbraeon Circle sees the Emblems not as divine gifts, but as dangerous anchors to a cyclical prison—the Recurrent Veil. They work in secret to dismantle the current world-order from the shadows, believing that only by destroying the Emblems can the world be truly free.

Symbol: A shattered halo coiled by serpents.

Methods: Infiltration, misinformation, theft of Emblem relics.

Beliefs: Reality is a veil spun by the Emblems themselves; true freedom lies in their unmaking.

Twist: Some high-ranking members of the Order may secretly be part of the Circle.

🩸 The Crimson Concord
“To awaken the sleeping gods, blood must be the ink.”

Overview:
A bloodbound society that believes the true potential of Emblems can only be unlocked through ancient rites and forbidden sacrifices. They operate beneath catacombs, old ruins, and lost cities, hiding behind noble houses or scholarly guilds.

Symbol: A blood-red quill writing upon an open eye.

Goal: To trigger a second Emblematic Awakening—a cataclysm that reshapes the world.

Connection: May have ties to Lyra’s or Damion’s ancestral bloodlines.

Reputation: Officially myth, whispered in folklore, with members using coded lullabies to identify each other.

🌒 The Eclipse Kin
“When the twin moons align, the world shall forget the sun.”

Overview:
A clandestine order operating during Eclipse Zones, times when dimensional veils thin. They believe Emblems are keys to realms beyond the mortal plane, and they experiment with dimensional travel, soul merging, and entity summoning.

Symbol: A pair of crescent moons crossing over a black sun.

Abilities: Masters of memory manipulation and Veilwalking.

Goal: To open permanent gateways to hidden realms, merging Terra-Mythica with its “mirrored self.”

Threat Level: High. They're responsible for the last known Veilbreach Crisis.
