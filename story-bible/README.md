# Story Bible

High-level reference material for *Shadows of the Veil: Awakening of the Emblematic* —
the stuff you'd hand a collaborator, editor, or your future self to get oriented fast.

- `overview.md` — concept recap: what the story/series is, at a glance
- `summaries.md` — several summary lengths/styles (200-word, showcase/pitch style, etc.)
- `themes-and-tone.md` — thematic pillars and mythic/archetypal patterns at work
- `character-roster.md` — full cast list, organized by role/faction
- `plot-events.md` — major arc events (e.g. the Crisis of Convergence)
- `editorial-plan.md` — manuscript editing/development plan
- `open-questions.md` — unresolved worldbuilding questions to answer over time
